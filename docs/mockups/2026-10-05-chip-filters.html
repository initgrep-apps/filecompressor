Run view — interactive chip filters (v5)

Chips become single-select filters (default All files). Counts always come from the full roster and never change when filtering. Lanes with zero matching rows are hidden. Rows that change status while filtered animate out. Two examples below: Failed filter (1 lane visible) and Queued filter (2 lanes, dashed rows only).
